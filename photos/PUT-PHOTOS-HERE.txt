PHOTOS GO IN THIS FOLDER
========================

The invitation looks for these exact filenames. Anything missing simply shows an
elegant empty frame with the caption, so nothing breaks while you collect them.

  us-01.jpg    "The first meeting"
  us-02.jpg    "Roka"
  us-03.jpg    "Both families"
  us-04.jpg    "That evening out"
  us-05.jpg    "Ring shopping"
  us-06.jpg    "Us"

  og-cover.jpg  The thumbnail WhatsApp shows when someone forwards the link.
                1200 x 630 px works best. This one really matters -- it is the
                first thing every guest sees, before they even open the page.

NOTES
  - Square crops look best in the hanging frames (they are displayed 1:1).
  - Keep each file under ~400 KB so the page stays quick on mobile data.
  - To change the captions, or to add/remove photos, edit CONFIG.gallery near
    the top of the <script> block in invitation.html.
  - Filenames are case sensitive on most web hosts. us-01.jpg is not Us-01.JPG.

Delete this file once your photos are in.
