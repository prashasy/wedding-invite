BACKGROUND MUSIC (optional)
===========================

Drop one audio file here, for example shehnai.mp3, then set this in
invitation.html:

    music: "audio/shehnai.mp3",

Leave it as "" and the music button is hidden entirely.

NOTES
  - Keep it under ~2 MB. It only starts after the guest taps the seal, which
    is what satisfies the browser's autoplay rules.
  - Guests can mute it any time with the button in the bottom-right corner.
  - Use something you have the right to use. Film songs on a public link are
    a copyright risk; instrumental shehnai or sitar is the safe route.
